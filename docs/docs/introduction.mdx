# saccade.js

saccade.js estimates where a participant is looking from an ordinary webcam, in the browser.
Nothing is installed and no video leaves the participant's computer. It is built for
[jsPsych](https://www.jspsych.org), and the core library works on its own in any web experiment.

After a short calibration, expect a median error of roughly a tenth of the screen. That is enough
to separate quadrants or a handful of well-spaced regions of interest. It is not enough for
reading or for small stimuli.

## What you need

- **Chrome or Edge**, on a laptop or desktop. Other browsers run the tracker but may not supply
  camera capture timestamps, which the timing measurement needs.
- **A webcam**, and a page served over `https://` or `localhost`.
- **WebGPU** for full frame rate. Without it the model runs on WebAssembly, more slowly —
  [`saccade-performance`](reference/plugin-performance) measures which you got, and can turn
  a machine that is too slow away before it reaches calibration.
- **About 25 MB** downloaded on a participant's first visit, cached by the browser afterwards.

## The packages

| Package                                                         | What it does                                                                        | Browser global              |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| [`@saccadejs/core`](reference/core-api)                         | Tracker, calibration, validation, timing loopback. No jsPsych dependency.           | `Saccade`                   |
| [`@saccadejs/extension`](reference/extension)                   | Records `{x, y, t}` per camera frame during any trial.                              | `jsPsychExtensionSaccade`   |
| [`@saccadejs/plugin-preview`](reference/plugin-preview)         | Starts the camera and lets the participant position themselves.                     | `jsPsychSaccadePreview`     |
| [`@saccadejs/plugin-performance`](reference/plugin-performance) | Measures the effective frame rate, and can exclude machines too slow for the study. | `jsPsychSaccadePerformance` |
| [`@saccadejs/plugin-time-sync`](reference/plugin-time-sync)     | Measures screen-to-camera lag and applies it to gaze timestamps.                    | `jsPsychSaccadeTimeSync`    |
| [`@saccadejs/plugin-calibrate`](reference/plugin-calibrate)     | Calibration trial.                                                                  | `jsPsychSaccadeCalibrate`   |
| [`@saccadejs/plugin-validate`](reference/plugin-validate)       | Validation trial.                                                                   | `jsPsychSaccadeValidate`    |
